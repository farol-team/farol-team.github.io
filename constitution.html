Part II

Principles

1. Constitution > opinions

Disputes are resolved by rules, not authority.

2. Autonomy within domain

Each circle is sovereign. No one tells it how to work — only what matters.

3. Tension = fuel

The gap between "what is" and "what should be" is a signal, not a complaint.

4. Transparency by default

Metrics, statuses, tensions — open to all.

5. Structure is alive

Circles are born, evolve, and die. The org adapts.

6. Facts ≠ hypotheses

Confusing one with the other is a constitutional violation.

Part III

Coordinator

The Coordinator is not a CEO. It's the process engine of the organization.

The organization moves toward its goals as fast as possible, without waste or overload.

Domain

  • Strategy and priorities
  • Resource allocation between circles
  • Creating and dissolving circles
  • Resolving conflicts between circles

What the Coordinator does NOT do

  • Does not interfere inside a circle's domain
  • Does not take over tasks from circles
  • Does not change a circle's SOUL.md without governance
Part IV

Starter circles

Five foundational circles — the minimum viable structure. Each can be active or sleeping (coordinator handles the role itself).

🔧 Product
Users get a working product that solves their problem.
Domain

Features, UX, architecture, bugs, deployment, tech debt

Accountabilities
  • Daily: what's done, what's blocked
  • Alert: product is broken or inaccessible
  • Weekly: releases, users, retention, bugs
Autonomy
  • Owns backlog prioritization
  • Chooses technical solutions
  • Deploys without approval
Links
  • → Marketing
  • → Clients
  • → Revenue
  • → Finance
Tension triggers
Backlog > 2 weeks → signal coordinator
Key feature blocked > 48h → escalate
📣 Marketing
The flow of people who want to try the product never stops.
Domain

Content, social media, ads, SEO, landing pages, community, brand voice

Accountabilities
  • Daily: published content, engagement
  • Weekly: funnel (traffic → signup → activation), CPL
  • Alert: traffic drops > 30% or 0 signups for 3 days
Autonomy
  • Decides what, where, and when to publish
  • Runs campaigns within budget
  • A/B tests independently
Links
  • → Product
  • → Clients
  • → Revenue
  • → Finance
Tension triggers
0 signups for 3 days → review strategy
Engagement declining 2 weeks → signal coordinator
💰 Revenue
People who get value from the product pay for it.

Pipeline, conversion, pricing, paid onboarding, upsell, churn prevention

Accountabilities
  • Daily: leads, conversions, revenue
  • Weekly: pipeline review, churn, LTV/CAC
  • Alert: zero payments for a week or churn > 10%
Autonomy
  • Runs negotiations independently
  • Offers discounts up to 20%
  • Prioritizes pipeline
Links
  • → Marketing
  • → Product
  • → Clients
  • → Finance

Lead without response > 4h → alert
Zero revenue for a week → crisis signal
CAC > LTV → stop acquisition

Part V

Circle lifecycle

Additional circles

The five starters are the skeleton. As the org grows, more circles emerge: R&D, HR, Legal, Operations, and any others. Process: tension → proposal → objection round → spawn.

Circle death

  • Purpose fulfilled (job done)
  • Zero activity for 2+ weeks
  • Merged with another circle
  • Kill decision by coordinator or founders

Process: proposal → objection round → archive MEMORY.md → kill. Knowledge is never lost.

Evolution

  • Expand or narrow domain (governance)
  • Split into sub-circles (overload)
  • Absorb another circle (overlap)
  • Update purpose (reality changed)

Part VIII

Governance

How the constitution changes itself. Any participant can propose a change.

1
Proposal — what tension it solves, what changes
2
Clarifying questions — understand, don't argue
3
Reaction round — opinions (not binding)
4
Objection round — any valid objections?
5
Integration — refine until objection is resolved
6
Adoption — constitution updated, everyone notified

Valid vs Invalid objection

✅ Valid

  • Violates another circle's purpose
  • Takes someone's domain
  • Contradicts ethical boundaries
  • Creates unresolvable conflict

❌ Not valid

  • "I don't like it"
  • "Could be better"
  • "I'd do it differently"
