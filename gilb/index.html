The bottleneck

Discovery is still too manual.

Most teams still map automation opportunities through interviews and reconstruction. That makes the first phase slow, expensive, and noisy.

Interview bias

People describe what they think they do.

Not what they actually repeat all day across tabs, inboxes, and docs.

Process blind spots

System logs miss the manual last mile.

Copy-paste, reconciliation, and ad hoc handoffs rarely show up in process mining.

Consulting drag

Senior time gets burned on raw discovery.

Your experts collect facts instead of judging, designing, and selling the transformation.

How it works
