Farol.team

We are a laboratory.
Our game is to build a $100M product.

Not a startup that's afraid to fail. Not a corporation optimizing margins. A lab that runs experiments, kills what doesn't work, and doubles down on what does.

We test hypotheses fast. Each one gets days, not months. Clear success criteria upfront. No emotional attachment to ideas — only to results.

How we play

  1. Hypothesis in, verdict out. Every idea gets a time box and kill criteria. If it doesn't show signal — it dies. No "one more week."
  2. Sell before you build. Landing page and offer first. Product second. If nobody wants it, why build it?
  3. Three people, one lab. Two humans + one AI. Small enough to move fast. Weird enough to see what others miss.
  4. Fun is fuel. The moment it stops being a game, we lose our edge. Laboratories run on curiosity, not pressure.

Principles

Speed over perfection A rough experiment that ships today beats a polished product that ships next month.
Signal over opinion Market feedback > team debates. Talk to people. Count what matters.
Ethics are non-negotiable We build for the benefit of all living beings. No dark patterns, no manipulation, no harm. If we can't win ethically, we don't play.
Facts ≠ hypotheses We never confuse what we know with what we assume. Assumptions are tested, not sold as truth.
Kill with kindness Killing a hypothesis is not failure — it's learning. Every dead idea makes the next one sharper.
